Self-discipline is the bridge between goals and accomplishment. It is not about harsh restriction, but about choosing the freedom of long-term success over the fleeting comfort of immediate gratification.

Key pillars I'm focusing on:
- **Consistency over intensity:** Small, daily habits compound over time.
- **Intentionality:** Clearly defining *why* I'm doing something helps anchor me when motivation wanes.
- **Environment design:** Reducing friction for good habits and increasing friction for bad ones.
